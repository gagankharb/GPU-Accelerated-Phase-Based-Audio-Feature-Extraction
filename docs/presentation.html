PROBLEM STATEMENT & MOTIVATION
Motivation: Moving Beyond Magnitude-Only Spectrograms
The Traditional Magnitude Approach
  • Standard front-ends (MFCCs, log-mel filterbanks) discard Fourier phase entirely.
  • Assumes human perception is phase-deaf, which holds only for steady-state sounds.
  • Fails to capture fine temporal structure, glottal pulse timing, and speech non-stationarity.
  • Serial CPU calculation of phase residuals introduces a major computational bottleneck.
The Phase Residual Advantage
  • Phase residuals preserve vocal tract excitation timing and instantaneous frequency.
  • Captures dynamic transitions across phoneme boundaries and room acoustic reflections.
  • Complementary to MFCCs: Combining magnitude and phase boosts acoustic classification.
  • Massive Parallelism: GPU transforms hundreds of frames simultaneously using cuFFT.
SIGNAL PROCESSING PIPELINE
Mathematical Formulation & 60-Feature Pipeline
15-Step Signal Processing Workflow
  • Audio Ingestion: 16 kHz 16-bit PCM (mono or downmixed stereo).
  • Overlapping Framing: L = 400 samples (25 ms), Hop H = 160 (10 ms).
  • Hann Windowing: Zero-padding to N = 512 points.
  • Batched cuFFT R2C: 512 real samples -> 257 complex bins.
  • Phase Extraction: atan2(Im, Re) for bins k = 0..255.
  • RMS Energy Gating: Suppresses random noise in silence frames.
  • Feature Output: 20 static + 20 delta + 20 delta-delta = 60 features.
Exact Mathematical Formulations
Phase Residual Equation:
Δφ(k, t) = wrap( φ(k, t) - φ(k, t-1) - 2πkH / N )
Modulo Wrapping to [-π, π]:
wrap(θ) = θ - 2π · round( θ / 2π )
Orthogonal Cepstral Projections:
c_n^cos(t) = (1/K) · Σ Δφ(k, t) · cos(π n (k+0.5) / K)
5-Point Temporal Delta Regression (D = 2):
Δc(t) = [ 1·(c(t+1)-c(t-1)) + 2·(c(t+2)-c(t-2)) ] / 10

LESSONS LEARNED & REFLECTIONS
Engineering Challenges Overcome & Lessons Learned
Key Engineering Takeaways
  • 1. Phase Unwrapping Discontinuities across Frames: Subtracting consecutive phases causes artificial 2π boundary wrapping. Subtracting the nominal linear phase advance (2πkH/N) prior to modulo wrapping ensures stationary harmonics yield stable zero residuals.
  • 2. Phase Noise Explosion in Silence & Low-Energy Gating: Near-zero background noise produces random uniform phase angles, causing massive spurious spikes. We designed an in-block shared memory tree reduction to compute frame RMS energy, gating silence branchlessly on the GPU.
  • 3. Intra-Warp Register Shuffles vs. Shared Memory: Reducing 256 frequency bins into 20 cepstral coefficients per frame caused shared-memory bank conflicts. Implementing __shfl_down_sync register shuffles eliminated shared-memory latency and synchronization overhead.
  • 4. Amdahl's Law & PCIe Transfer Bottlenecks: For short clips (<1s), PCIe transfers take 34% of runtime. Processing audio in larger batches increases the compute-to-transfer ratio, allowing the GPU to approach its theoretical computational throughput peak.
